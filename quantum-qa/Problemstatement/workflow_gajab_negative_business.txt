Test Case Pack: Gajab Business-Critical Negative Scenarios

Website: https://stg.gajab.com/
Test Data:
- Valid OTP: '123456'
- Invalid OTP: '111111'
- Mobile Number: random 10 digit number
- Name: Test User
- Gender: Male
- Non-serviceable Pin Code: '000000'

Scenario 1: Block OTP request when Terms and Conditions is not accepted
Business Impact: Prevents non-compliant account creation and legal exposure.
1. Launch browser
2. Navigate to url 'https://stg.gajab.com/'
3. Verify that home page is visible
4. Click on 'Log in' or 'Sign up' button
5. Enter random 10 digit mobile number in mobile field
6. Click 'Request OTP' button
7. Verify terms and conditions error message is visible
8. Verify sign in page is visible

Scenario 2: Reject invalid OTP and prevent account creation
Business Impact: Prevents fraudulent or unauthorized account access.
9. Navigate to url 'https://stg.gajab.com/'
10. Click on 'Log in' or 'Sign up' button
11. Enter random 10 digit mobile number in mobile field
12. Click on the check box for terms and conditions
13. Click 'Request OTP' button
14. Enter OTP '111111' in OTP field
15. Click on 'Submit' or 'Next' button to verify OTP
16. Verify invalid OTP error message is visible
17. Verify sign in page is visible

Scenario 3: Block unsupported location pin code
Business Impact: Prevents failed fulfillment and reduces return/cancellation risk.
18. Navigate to url 'https://stg.gajab.com/'
